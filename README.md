# Project-2
Second project for Terra Hack Club: This is a text-based casino game written in Python. You start by depositing money, then choose between two games from a menu: a three-by-three slot machine and blackjack. In slots, you pick how many lines to bet on (three rows and two diagonals) and how much to put on each, and the symbols spin with a colourful animation, with wild cards that stand in for any symbol and highlights on the winning squares. You can also auto-spin up to 100 times or bet the maximum with one word. In blackjack, you play against a dealer who stands on 17: you can hit, stand or double down, and a natural blackjack pays 3 to 2. Your balance carries between both games, and a statistics screen tracks your wins, losses, biggest results and how much of your money came back to you. The game ends when you quit or run out of money.
